# Boogie Jazzy Rock Prompt Variants

## 方針
- 同じ世界観のまま、重みを少しずつ変えた複数プロンプトを使う
- ブギウギ、ジャズ、ロック、反復、展開、弾きやすさの軸をずらす
- 後で一括生成しやすいように、短いラベルを付ける

## Variants

### 1. balanced_core
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece. Keep a strong repetitive groove and a clear core rhythm throughout the piece. Start with a sparse texture and gradually add more notes, thicker chords, syncopated accents, bluesy fills, and energetic embellishments while preserving the same rhythmic foundation. The music should blend boogie-woogie drive, jazzy harmony, rock energy, and fusion-like richness. It should feel increasingly luxurious, exciting, and full over time, but remain playable by a human pianist.`

### 2. jazz_heavy
`A solo piano boogie-woogie inspired jazzy rock fusion piece with stronger jazz harmony. Keep a repetitive groove and a stable pulse, but emphasize extended chords, bluesy passing tones, chromatic color, and stylish syncopation. Begin simply and gradually become richer and more elegant while keeping the same rhythmic foundation. It should sound sophisticated, groovy, and human-playable.`

### 3. rock_heavy
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with stronger rock energy. Keep the rhythm repetitive and driving, with a clear pulse and a forceful groove. Start sparse, then build by adding thicker chord stabs, more aggressive rhythmic accents, and energetic right-hand riffs while preserving the core pattern. Strong, exciting, and still playable by a human pianist.`

### 4. boogie_heavy
`A solo piano piece with strong boogie-woogie character, mixed with jazzy rock fusion color. Use a rolling repetitive groove, blues-based motion, and a lively left-hand engine. Let the right hand become gradually richer with riffs, fills, and chord color, but keep the boogie pulse dominant the whole time. Bright, driving, playful, and human-playable.`

### 5. repetition_heavy
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece built on a strongly repetitive rhythmic pattern. Keep the same core groove repeating clearly across the whole piece, and make the music evolve mostly by adding notes, decorations, harmony extensions, and dynamic weight rather than changing the basic rhythm. Hypnotic, groovy, stylish, and playable by a human pianist.`

### 6. rhythm_looser
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with a slightly looser rhythmic feel. Maintain the main groove, but allow more breathing room, swung accents, and flexible syncopation. Start sparse and gradually add color, density, and expressive phrasing while keeping a recognizable repeated rhythmic base. Groovy, expressive, and human-playable.`

### 7. gradual_build_heavy
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with a very strong gradual build. Begin with a minimal groove and simple harmony, then steadily increase note density, chord thickness, rhythmic activity, and melodic ornamentation in each section. Keep the original pulse and repeated foundation intact while the texture grows more luxurious and full. Human-playable and exciting.`

### 8. playable_focus
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece designed to be clearly playable by a human pianist. Keep a repetitive groove, controlled hand movement, moderate chord sizes, and a natural build from simple texture to richer texture. Use jazzy color, bluesy movement, and rock-like energy, but avoid extreme jumps and excessive density.`

### 9. flashy_fusion
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with a glamorous fusion flavor. Keep a repeated groove and strong pulse, then gradually add brilliant chord colors, fast embellishments, syncopated phrases, and a luxurious concert-like finish. The piece should feel bold, stylish, and exciting, but still playable by a skilled human pianist.`

### 10. bluesy_groove
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with stronger blues feeling. Keep a repetitive groove and steady pulse, use bluesy turns, crunchy chord color, and expressive fills, and gradually intensify the texture while preserving the same rhythmic base. Earthy, groovy, energetic, and playable by a human pianist.`

### 11. left_hand_engine
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece driven by a strong repeating left-hand groove. Let the left hand act like an engine, while the right hand gradually adds riffs, chord tones, syncopated hooks, and decorative lines. Keep the foundation consistent, but make the full texture become richer and more powerful over time. Human-playable.`

### 12. right_hand_riffs
`A solo piano boogie-woogie jazzy rock fusion piece with catchy right-hand riffs over a repetitive groove. Keep the pulse clear and repeating, start with a simple rhythmic motif, and gradually enrich it with thicker harmony, bluesy fills, and more energetic fusion-style phrasing. Stylish, rhythmic, and playable by a human pianist.`
