# Easy Listening Prompt Variants

## 方針
- 耳に刺さる高音や強い打鍵感を避ける
- 温かい中低域、滑らかな和声、穏やかな反復を重視する
- 12 曲すべてを同じ `max_tokens` で生成して長さを揃える

## Variants

### 1. warm_balanced
`A solo acoustic piano easy-listening piece with a warm, balanced tone. Keep a gentle repeating pulse, smooth voice leading, and rounded chords throughout. Build slowly from a simple texture into a slightly richer one without becoming sharp or busy. Relaxed, polished, and pleasant to hear.`

### 2. lounge_jazz
`A solo acoustic piano lounge-jazz piece with a soft groove and a mellow sound. Keep the rhythm steady and understated, use tasteful extended chords, and avoid bright or piercing high notes. Let the harmony feel smooth, calm, and refined while staying comfortably playable.`

### 3. soft_bossa
`A solo acoustic piano piece with a soft bossa-inspired easy-listening feel. Keep the pulse light, flowing, and unobtrusive, with gentle syncopation and warm chord colors. Add subtle motion over time, but keep the overall sound relaxed and airy.`

### 4. gentle_boogie
`A solo acoustic piano piece with only a subtle boogie pulse, softened into an easy-listening style. Keep the left hand steady and warm, and let the right hand stay melodic, rounded, and uncluttered. Use bluesy color lightly, without harsh accents or excessive drive.`

### 5. evening_flow
`A solo acoustic piano easy-listening piece that feels like a calm evening flow. Keep a repeating foundation, smooth transitions, and a balanced texture that grows gradually. Avoid sharp attacks, crowded passages, and bright upper-register flashes.`

### 6. mellow_waltz
`A solo acoustic piano piece with a gentle waltz-like sway and a mellow easy-listening mood. Keep the motion graceful and even, with soft chord changes and clear melodic shapes. The music should feel soothing, elegant, and unforced.`

### 7. gradual_warm_build
`A solo acoustic piano easy-listening piece with a very gradual warm build. Start sparse and intimate, then add a little harmonic color, richer accompaniment, and slightly fuller phrases over time. Keep the energy low-pressure and the timbre soft.`

### 8. playable_smooth
`A solo acoustic piano piece designed for smooth, comfortable listening and easy playability. Keep chord sizes moderate, register changes modest, and the touch rounded. Use tasteful jazz harmony, but avoid dense clusters and aggressive peaks.`

### 9. polished_lounge
`A solo acoustic piano polished lounge piece with a clean, calm surface. Keep the groove stable, the harmony silky, and the melody unhurried. Let the arrangement feel balanced and cohesive from start to finish.`

### 10. bluesy_soft
`A solo acoustic piano easy-listening piece with a soft blues tint. Keep the blues feeling tasteful and restrained, using warm passing tones and gentle fills rather than sharp riffs. The sound should stay smooth, relaxed, and friendly to the ear.`

### 11. left_hand_warmth
`A solo acoustic piano piece driven by a warm, repetitive left-hand pattern. Let the left hand provide a soft engine while the right hand adds calm melodic phrases and light harmonic color. Keep the overall feeling mellow, even, and unobtrusive.`

### 12. melody_first
`A solo acoustic piano easy-listening piece with a melody-first approach. Keep the accompaniment simple and supportive, and let the tune stay clear, lyrical, and soothing. Build the texture gently without introducing harshness or excess density.`
